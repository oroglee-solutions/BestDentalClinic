What These Treatments Look Like in Practice

Every case here is anonymised, and every result depends on the individual — bone quality, gum health, habits and how closely aftercare is followed all change the outcome.

Implants

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Male, 58 · 8 months

The problem
Years of worn-down and missing back teeth had collapsed the bite, causing jaw pain and difficulty chewing anything firm.
What we did
Six implants placed across both arches, followed by a full set of zirconia restorations rebuilt to the correct vertical height.
The result
Chewing function restored across both sides, jaw pain resolved, and a bite that no longer wears itself down.
About this treatment
Orthodontics

Adult Clear Aligner Correction

Female, 31 · 14 months

The problem
Moderate crowding in the lower front teeth with relapse from braces worn as a teenager, and reluctance to wear visible appliances at work.
What we did
22 sets of clear aligners with attachments on four teeth, planned digitally and reviewed every six weeks.
The result
Crowding fully resolved, midline corrected, and fixed lingual retainers placed to prevent a second relapse.
About this treatment
Implants

Single Tooth Implant, Front Zone

Male, 34 · 5 months

The problem
Upper front tooth fractured below the gum line after a sports injury and could not be saved.
What we did
Atraumatic extraction with immediate socket grafting, implant placed after healing, then a layered ceramic crown shade-matched to the adjacent tooth.
The result
The restored tooth is indistinguishable from its neighbour in normal light, with the gum contour preserved.
About this treatment
Cosmetic

Smile Makeover with Veneers

Female, 27 · 3 weeks

The problem
Uneven tooth widths, two chipped incisors and a shade the patient had never been happy with.
What we did
Digital smile design, a trial mock-up worn for a week, then eight minimal-preparation porcelain veneers on the upper arch.
The result
Even proportions and a natural shade gradation — the patient approved the exact look before any enamel was touched.
About this treatment
Emergency

Emergency Root Canal & Crown

Female, 44 · 3 weeks

The problem
Severe throbbing pain in a lower molar that had kept the patient awake for three nights, with early facial swelling.
What we did
Same-day emergency root canal to relieve pressure and remove the infected tissue, followed by a zirconia crown three weeks later.
The result
Pain resolved within hours of the first visit, and the tooth was saved rather than extracted.
About this treatment
Surgery

Impacted Wisdom Tooth Removal

Male, 25 · Single visit

The problem
A horizontally impacted lower wisdom tooth causing repeated gum infections and pressing against the molar in front.
What we did
Surgical removal under local anaesthesia with sectioning of the tooth to minimise bone removal.
The result
Infections stopped completely and the adjacent molar was preserved. Normal eating resumed within four days.
About this treatment
Pediatric

Children's Preventive Programme

Two siblings, 6 and 9 · 2 years of reviews

The problem
Early decay in four molars across two siblings, with a first visit that had gone badly at another clinic.
What we did
A no-treatment familiarisation visit first, then tooth-coloured fillings, sealants on all permanent molars, and a diet review with the parents.
The result
No new cavities across two years of six-monthly reviews, and both children now attend without complaint.
About this treatment
Cosmetic

Cleaning & Whitening Refresh

Female, 38 · 4 weeks

The problem
Heavy tea and coffee staining with bleeding gums, and a wedding six weeks away.
What we did
Full scaling and root planing to resolve the gum inflammation first, then in-clinic whitening once the gums were healthy.
The result
Bleeding resolved within two weeks and the shade lifted noticeably, maintained afterwards with a take-home top-up kit.
About this treatment
Restorative

Crowns for a Cracked Tooth Pattern

Male, 49 · 6 weeks

The problem
Repeated cracks in heavily filled back teeth caused by long-term night grinding.
What we did
Zirconia crowns on three compromised molars to take the load off the remaining tooth structure, plus a custom night guard.
The result
No further fractures across three years of reviews, and the night guard is now absorbing the wear instead of the teeth.
About this treatment
