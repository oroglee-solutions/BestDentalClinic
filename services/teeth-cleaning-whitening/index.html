Treatment Overview

Brushing cannot remove hardened tartar, and tartar below the gum line is what drives gum disease — the leading cause of adult tooth loss in India. Professional scaling removes it properly.

Cleaning and whitening are two different things. Scaling removes deposits and surface stain to reveal your actual tooth shade; whitening then lightens that underlying shade itself.

We always clean first. Whitening applied over tartar and plaque gives patchy results and irritates the gums.

The Process

What to Expect, Step by Step

1

Gum Assessment

We chart pocket depths and bleeding points to see how much cleaning is actually needed.

2

Ultrasonic Scaling

Deposits are removed above and below the gum line, with anaesthesia if the gums are inflamed.

3

Polish & Fluoride

Surface stain is polished away and fluoride applied to reduce post-cleaning sensitivity.

4

Whitening (optional)

Once the teeth are clean, the whitening gel is applied with the gums fully protected.
