Treatment Overview

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once the bone fuses to it, the post supports a crown, bridge or denture that is fixed in place — looking, feeling and functioning like a natural tooth.

Because the implant replaces the root and not just the visible tooth, it keeps the jawbone stimulated. That prevents the bone shrinkage and facial collapse that follows long-term tooth loss.

Unlike a conventional bridge, an implant does not require grinding down the healthy teeth on either side of the gap.

Your Options

Approaches We Offer

We recommend the option that fits your case, not the most expensive one on the list.

01

Single Tooth Implant

One post and one crown replaces one missing tooth, without touching the neighbouring teeth.

02

Implant Bridge

Two implants support a bridge spanning three or four missing teeth in a row.

03

All-on-4 / All-on-6

A full arch of fixed teeth supported on four to six implants, for patients missing most or all teeth.

04

Implant-Supported Denture

A removable denture that clips securely onto implants instead of resting on the gums.

The Process

What to Expect, Step by Step

1

Assessment & Imaging

We check bone volume, gum health and bite with digital imaging before recommending implants.

2

Implant Placement

The post is placed under local anaesthesia in a single sitting, typically 45–60 minutes.

3

Healing & Integration

The bone fuses to the implant over 3 to 6 months. A temporary tooth bridges the gap.

4

Final Crown Fitted

An abutment and custom crown are fitted, shade-matched to the rest of your smile.
