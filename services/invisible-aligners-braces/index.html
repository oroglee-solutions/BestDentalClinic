Treatment Overview

What Invisible Aligners & Braces Involves

Crowded, gapped or protruding teeth are harder to clean, wear unevenly and often make people hide their smile. Orthodontic treatment fixes the position of the teeth themselves, which fixes both problems at once.

We offer clear removable aligners as well as metal, ceramic and self-ligating braces. Which one suits you depends on how much movement is needed, how fast you want results, and how visible you're willing for the appliance to be.

Every case starts with a digital scan and a treatment simulation, so you can see the projected end result before committing to anything.

Key Benefits

Why Patients Choose This Treatment

Virtually invisible

Clear aligners are barely noticeable in conversation or photographs.

Removable for meals

Take aligners out to eat and to brush — no food restrictions at all.

Digital treatment planning shows the projected result up front.

Your Options

Approaches We Offer

We recommend the option that fits your case, not the most expensive one on the list.

01

Clear Aligners

A series of custom transparent trays worn 20–22 hours a day. Best for mild to moderate crowding, spacing and relapse cases.

02

Metal Braces

The most cost-effective and versatile option. Handles severe crowding, bite correction and complex rotations.

03

Ceramic Braces

Tooth-coloured brackets that blend in far better than metal, with the same clinical capability.

04

Retainers

Worn after active treatment to hold the corrected position. Skipping retainers is the main cause of relapse.

No-cost EMI available

Spread the cost of major treatment over easy monthly instalments. Ask at the front desk for the current plans.

The Process

What to Expect, Step by Step

1

Consultation & Scan

Photographs, digital scan and X-rays to record exactly where your teeth sit today.

2

Treatment Simulation

We show you the projected movement and end result, along with duration and cost.

3

Appliance Fitted

Aligners are handed over with a wear schedule, or braces are bonded in a single sitting.

4

Reviews & Retention

Progress checks every 4–8 weeks, then retainers to lock in the final position.
