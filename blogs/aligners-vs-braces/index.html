Aligners vs Braces: An Honest Comparison

Dr. Vidya Kadiyala 10 July 2026 7 min read

Clear aligners are not automatically better — and metal braces are not automatically cheaper in the long run. What actually decides it is your case.

Almost every adult who asks about straightening their teeth opens with the same question: can I do it with the invisible ones? Sometimes the answer is yes. Often the more useful answer is that it depends on what needs to move, and by how much.

Where aligners genuinely win

Clear aligners are removable, so you eat normally and brush normally. That single fact resolves most of the hygiene problems that come with fixed braces, and it is why aligner patients often finish treatment with healthier gums than they started with.

They are also comfortable. There are no brackets to irritate the cheek and no wire to snap on a Sunday evening. Review appointments are typically every six to eight weeks rather than every four.

For mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and relapse after previous orthodontic work, the clinical outcome is comparable to braces.

Where braces still win

Fixed appliances give the orthodontist far more control over difficult movements: severe rotations, teeth that need bodily movement through bone, significant bite correction, and closing large extraction spaces. Aligners can be pushed to do some of this with attachments and elastics, but the predictability drops.

Braces also remove the compliance problem entirely. Aligners only work if they are in the mouth 20 to 22 hours a day. For teenagers, and for adults with demanding schedules, that is a real risk — treatment stretches out and the result drifts from the plan.

The cost question

Aligners generally cost more than metal braces for equivalent treatment. Ceramic braces sit in between. But comparing headline prices misses two things: the number of refinement trays a complex aligner case needs, and the cost of extending treatment when trays are not worn properly.

Ask for the total treatment cost including refinements and retainers, not the starting figure. Any clinic should give you that in writing.

The part nobody mentions

Retainers are not optional. Teeth have a memory and will drift back toward their original position for the rest of your life if nothing holds them. This applies equally to aligners and braces. Budget for retainers, wear them as instructed, and replace them when they wear out.

How to decide

Get a digital scan and a treatment simulation before you commit. A proper assessment will tell you whether aligners can deliver your specific result, and any clinic confident in its work will tell you when they cannot.
